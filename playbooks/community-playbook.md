# Instagram → WhatsApp Community Playbook

**Timelabs Co · working document · v1 — 12 Jul 2026**

The funnel in one line: a prospect DMs on Instagram → we answer their question *and* offer the richer WhatsApp channel → their first WhatsApp exchange welcomes them and invites them into the community → the community nurtures them until they order.

---

## 1. The funnel map

| Stage | Where | What happens | Asset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Instagram post/ad/reel | Prospect DMs a question | — |
| First touch | IG DM | Instant reply answers within seconds, offers WhatsApp | Snippet A |
| Handoff | wa.me link | One tap opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message that tells us the source | Snippet B |
| Welcome | WhatsApp 1:1 | Founder welcome: trust facts + one open question + community invite | Snippet C |
| Nurture | Community group | 1–2 high-signal posts weekly until they're ready to order | Section 5 |
| Order | WhatsApp 1:1 | Spec card → token advance → build photos → dispatch | existing flow |

**Why this shape:** links are clickable in IG DMs for business accounts; the pre-filled wa.me text doubles as source attribution with zero tooling; and for ₹20k+ purchases the first message's job is to reduce uncertainty, not to sell.

## 2. Snippet A — Instagram instant reply

Set in **Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations → Instant reply → toggle Instagram on**. Fires once per new conversation.

> Hey, Schezan here from Timelabs 🙂 Thanks for the message — I personally reply to every DM, usually within a few hours. Meanwhile: current builds and pricing are in our Highlights. If you'd like build photos, videos and availability faster, I run everything on WhatsApp — tap and say hi: https://wa.me/917776960608?text=Hi%20Schezan%2C%20came%20from%20Instagram

**Craft notes:** answers first, offers the channel second; sells the *benefit* of WhatsApp (photos/videos/availability faster), never "message me there to order"; under 500 characters; one emoji — restraint reads as premium.

Save as a WhatsApp quick reply. Send as the first response to any new prospect:

**To confirm before go-live:** exact warranty wording (site currently says "limited" — competitors lead with 1–2 year movement warranties; decide and state it here), and the official community invite link.

## 5. Community structure & pinned welcome

Structure for a solo founder: **one announcement group (admin-only posts) + one open subgroup** ("The Bench" — wrist shots, mod questions). Don't add more subgroups until the first is genuinely active. Announcement groups reach up to 5,000 members.

Pinned welcome / group description:

> Welcome to the Timelabs Collectors Circle. What you get here: first look at new builds before Instagram, member-only pricing on select drops, and behind-the-bench progress shots. I post 1–2 updates a week — no spam, ever. Wrist shots and mod questions go in The Bench. To start a build, message me directly. Response hours: 10am–8pm IST. — Schezan

**Cadence rules (what kills communities):** more than 2 promos a week → people mute; extended silence → people forget why they joined; new members with no welcome → lurkers forever. 1–2 high-signal posts weekly, that's it.

## 6. Four-week content calendar (repeatable)

| Week | Post 1 | Post 2 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | New build reveal — 3 photos + one-line story of the build | Poll: "Which dial next?" (2 options) |
| 2 | Behind the bench — movement/assembly shot, 60-sec video if possible | Member spotlight: repost a customer wrist shot (with permission) |
| 3 | Drop announcement — one model, member pricing, limited units | Q&A: answer the week's most-asked DM question publicly |
| 4 | Care tip — strap care / movement winding / water resistance reality | Teaser: next month's build, one detail photo only |

Rotate monthly. Every post ends with a soft handle: "want one like this? message me."

## 7. Measurement (the version a solo founder actually sustains)

- Track exactly **three numbers, weekly**: IG DMs received → WhatsApp chats started → orders closed.
- Source comes free from the pre-filled first message (Section 3).
- For anyone who arrives without a tag, ask in message 2–3: *"Quick one — where did you find us?"* (free text beats options).
- Log: star the WhatsApp messages during the week, batch-update one sheet every Monday (daily logging dies; weekly survives). Or simply tell the agent: "note: 4 joins this week, 3 from IG story" — it goes to the database and the Monday review picks it up.

## 8. Owner setup checklist (one sitting, ~25 minutes)

1. **Confirm the community invite link** (now: https://chat.whatsapp.com/COyrXIvS3Lj957IMe5IwK9?mode=gi_t) and replace [COMMUNITY_LINK] everywhere. *(2 min)*
2. **Decide the warranty statement** and update Snippet C + product pages. *(5 min)*
3. **IG instant reply:** Meta Business Suite → Inbox → Automations → Instant reply → paste Snippet A. *(5 min)*
4. **IG FAQs (ice breakers):** IG app → Settings → Business tools → Frequently asked questions → add 4 (price range, delivery time, custom builds, warranty) — 80 chars per question, 500 per answer, shown to first-time DMers. *(5 min)*
5. **Saved replies:** IG → Settings → Business tools → Saved replies → add Snippets A/C variants; in any DM type "/" to insert. *(3 min)*
6. **Order-confirmation email:** Shopify admin → Settings → Notifications → Order confirmation → add the community invite line with the order-email wa.me link. *(5 min)*
7. Pin the welcome in the community and post the Week-1 reveal.
