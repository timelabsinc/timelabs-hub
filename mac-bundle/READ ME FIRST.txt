⌚ TIMELABS CONTROL — SETUP (one time only)

1. Double-click "Install Timelabs Control.command"
   (If Mac blocks it: right-click the file → Open → Open.)

2. That's it. Two new icons appear on your Desktop:

   • Timelabs Control    — double-click to connect. Your browser opens:
                            - Hermes (your AI agent)      http://localhost:9119
                            - Ops Dashboard (business #s) http://localhost:8123/ops/
   • Disconnect Timelabs — closes the connection.

3. Delete this folder after installing — it contains your
   access key and shouldn't sit in Downloads.

Notes
- The dashboard asks for a login: user "timelabs", your ops password.
- Your access key (~/.ssh/timelabs_hermes) IS your login to the server.
  Don't share it. If your Mac is ever lost/stolen, tell your admin (or
  Claude) to revoke the key — one line on the server locks it out.
