# TimeLabs competitive intelligence audit

**Prepared:** 4 August 2026  
**Scope:** India-first custom/mod watches, adjacent Indian microbrands, substitute brands, discovery channels, TimeLabs public storefront and connected Instagram.  
**Method:** Hermes/Composio read-only Instagram/API work plus independent public web research. No blocked account was bypassed; no private competitor analytics, DMs, customer data or credentials were accessed.

## Executive answer

The earlier work was incomplete, not lost context. It over-focused on one competitor and did not separate direct mod sellers from the larger set of Indian brands competing for the same enthusiast budget. This audit expands the market map and identifies the practical gaps TimeLabs can close.

The category is not won by catalogue size alone. It is a trust-and-proof market with three visible buying paths:

1. **Low/mid-price mod discovery:** IndiaModWatches, AG WatchStudio, SeikoMods India, WatchModCustom and Instagram-first builders.
2. **Story-led Indian microbrands:** Delhi Watch Company, Coromandel Watch Co., Ajwain, Kala, Rotoris, Jaipur Watch Company, Bangalore Watch Company and others.
3. **Education/community substitutes:** Ghadicode, Reddit watch communities, YouTube creators, Google search and WhatsApp/Instagram consultations.

TimeLabs already has a good product foundation and a WhatsApp route, but its public acquisition system is under-instrumented: only five Instagram posts appeared in the 5 May–3 August window, the profile link points to a WhatsApp catalogue rather than a measured landing page, and the store repeats broad claims without turning each build into a visible, serialised proof asset. The immediate opportunity is not “post more” in isolation; it is a measurable Reel → proof carousel → WhatsApp consultation → build record → owner review loop.

## Direct competitor dossiers

### IndiaModWatches / @imodwatches

This is the closest direct benchmark. The storefront spans Seiko/HMT-derived modifications and homage-style families, with visible “under ₹3,000 / ₹5,000 / ₹15,000” navigation, a ₹4,620 entry automatic and premium-looking products around ₹17,999–₹19,999. Product pages claim NH35/NH36/NH38/VK63 options, custom parts, 9–12 or 15–20 working-day promises and a two-year machine warranty on ₹15,000+ models. The site-linked Instagram is `@imodwatches`; older public Reddit references use `@india_mod_watches`, but the relationship is not independently proven.

The conversion strengths are price anchoring, catalogue breadth, scarcity language and WhatsApp support. The risk opening is proof: public Reddit discussions are anecdotal and mixed around provenance, “Franken” construction, movement age/accuracy and after-sales support. These are signals to address, not verified findings: [storefront](https://indiamodwatch.in/), [Joker product](https://indiamodwatch.in/products/seiko-joker-dual-time-automatic-homage-mod-black-purple), [refund policy](https://indiamodwatch.in/policies/refund-policy), [shipping policy](https://indiamodwatch.in/policies/shipping-policy), [public discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/watchesindia/comments/1f14gaf).

### AG WatchStudio

AG is a high-overlap, trust-led Instagram/store competitor. Its public catalogue shows DayDate/Miyota 8285 at ₹18,999, Daytona at ₹18,500, Aquanaut NH35 builds around ₹17,000–₹18,999, Cubitus at ₹21,999 and VK63 Daytona/Seitona at ₹17,500. Its FAQ says watches are assembled, tested and photographed by Abhishek and Apoorva in Kanpur, shipping is free, general timelines are 4–6 weeks and returns are limited to defect replacement. It also publishes a “Not AI Written” guide, directly using education and authenticity as a differentiator: [AG storefront](https://agwatchstudio.in/), [AG guide](https://agwatchstudio.in/blogs/guides).

TimeLabs is directly in AG’s visible price band. Competing on the same product nouns will blur both brands; competing on a public build passport, consistent QC evidence and faster, more explicit customer updates creates separation.

### SeikoMods India

This newly launched Indian brand positions itself around premium design and craftsmanship without excessive luxury pricing. Its visible bestsellers include Crimson Valentine, Chocolate Arabic Dual Tone, Monarch Blue Roman and Ice Blue Roman, with a direct Instagram/email/phone route and a 10% launch-style code visible in public search. Its advantage is a cleaner brand presentation and named collections; its risk is limited observed proof and public history: [SeikoMods India](https://seikomodsindia.com/).

### WatchModCustom and global benchmarks

WatchModCustom advertises exclusive imagery and an online configurator, addressing the customisation gap directly: [WatchModCustom](https://www.watchmodcustom.com/). ModTime makes the consultation route explicit: one-to-one design guidance, WhatsApp as the fastest path and clear non-affiliation language: [ModTime custom consultation](https://modtime.store/pages/custom-seiko-mod-watches). SVK Watches shows a mature version of the same model—personalised quote, build photos/videos, 2–3 week production and a two-year warranty—while also using YouTube for education; its public page lists about 14,800 YouTube subscribers: [SVK Watches](https://svkwatches.ae/). These are useful operating benchmarks even when they are not India-based.

## Adjacent Indian microbrand set

These brands are not all direct mod sellers. They compete for the same watch-enthusiast attention, gifting budget and credibility expectations.

- **Delhi Watch Company:** accessible, India-themed mechanical and quartz designs; strong under-₹20k consideration.
- **Coromandel Watch Co.:** heritage and regional story-led designs; recent community conversation names Coromandel among the most impressive 2026 releases.
- **Ajwain Watches:** Mumbai-rooted cultural/design storytelling and collector drops.
- **Kala Watch Co.:** art/craft-led editions, including Warli references in curated guides.
- **Rotoris:** premium mechanical and design-led positioning.
- **Jaipur Watch Company:** coins, miniature/Pichwai art and bespoke Indian storytelling at a higher price ladder.
- **Bangalore Watch Company:** aviation, space and cricket storyworlds with a more premium “serious microbrand” posture.
- **Mangalore Watch Company, Sylvi, Horpa and Argos:** additional accessible/enthusiast alternatives surfaced in Indian microbrand guides.

The strategic lesson is that “Indian-made/Indian-story” is already a competitive language. TimeLabs should claim its own maker vocabulary—bench, components, regulation, ownership—rather than borrow generic “premium custom watches” wording.

## TimeLabs storefront audit

Evidence is from the live public storefront [timelabsco.in](https://timelabsco.in/), not an assumed staging copy.

### Strengths

- Clear top-level promises: hand-built custom watches, authentic Seiko movements and global shipping.
- A visible catalogue with named collections and a price band around ₹18,500–₹23,500 for several hero builds.
- Strong social proof claims: 99.5% satisfaction, 1,000+ owners, verified-buyer testimonials and five-star/modder language.
- A “read before you buy” section already explains custom assembly, aftermarket components, water-resistance limits, processing/delivery windows and support.
- WhatsApp Community and email capture are present in the footer, so the channel foundation exists.

### High-priority flaws or friction

1. **Proof is asserted more often than demonstrated.** “Authentic Japanese movements,” satisfaction rates and “recognised for best quality” need linked evidence: movement reference, QC date, regulation result, pressure-test/water-resistance scope, build photos and maker initials.
2. **The customer has to reconcile many names.** The navigation has a very broad Seiko-style taxonomy (SEIJUST, SEITONA, SEIKONAUT, SEIKOMASTER, etc.). It is useful for search coverage but weak for a first-time buyer. Add “Shop by budget,” “Shop by wrist style,” “Ready to ship” and “Custom commission” entry points.
3. **Copy quality leaks trust.** The live page contains visible wording/typo problems such as “Authetntic” and “ATLEAST.” Fix all copy, then add a single plain-language terminology guide explaining mod, custom, donor/base and aftermarket.
4. **Testimonials appear repeated in the page source.** Repetition makes social proof feel templated. Keep a smaller rotating set with date, product, photo permission and a link to the relevant build.
5. **The conversion destination is not measured.** The Instagram bio currently resolves to a WhatsApp catalogue link. Replace it or wrap it with a trackable landing page that records campaign/content source before handing off to WhatsApp.
6. **Water-resistance language is easy to misread.** Keep the conservative warning, but put the exact tested/untested status beside every product and in the checkout acknowledgement.
7. **The category names can create affiliation confusion.** Continue the clear non-affiliation disclosure at product level, not only in a long FAQ; make the brand identity and custom nature prominent before checkout.

## Hermes/Composio Instagram audit

The connected account is active and returned the managed profile `@timelabs.co` as a BUSINESS account with 3,117 followers and 91 media items. The profile currently says “Buy once, wear forever,” advertises a two-year warranty and sends users to a WhatsApp catalogue.

For 5 May–3 August 2026, Meta returned only `reach` and `follower_count` at account level; the other requested series were omitted by Meta for this window. The returned reach time series shows an early spike around 5–10 May, almost no reach through late June, then a steadier July baseline. This is a data-quality limitation, not proof that every unreported metric was zero.

Only five media items were returned in the window. Native post insights ranked them as follows:

| Date | Format | Build | Views | Reach | Interactions | Saves | Shares |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 7 May | Reel | Rosegold Rainbow | 4,399 | 3,412 | 101 | 18 | 22 |
| 9 May | Reel | John Mayor Gold Green | 1,940 | 1,470 | 30 | 4 | 1 |
| 30 Jul | Carousel | Ice Blue Translucent Case | 1,307 | 506 | 29 | 2 | 1 |
| 7 May | Carousel | Rosegold Rainbow | 872 | 406 | 18 | 2 | 1 |
| 9 May | Carousel | John Mayor Gold Green | 931 | 396 | 15 | 0 | 0 |

The strongest signal is format-plus-subject, not merely posting frequency: the Rosegold Rainbow Reel produced roughly 2.1× the views of the paired carousel and materially more saves/shares. Reels should be the discovery layer; the follow-up carousel should explain movement, materials, price, warranty, lead time and the consultation CTA.

### Instagram gaps to fix

- Five posts in 90 days is too thin to learn reliably or maintain discovery.
- Captions are primarily product/spec/DM prompts; they lack repeated educational hooks, owner stories, objections, proof and explicit source tracking.
- There is no visible content series for “inside the build,” accuracy/regulation, water-resistance limits, warranty claims, delivery stages or aftercare.
- A WhatsApp handoff exists, but there is no measured source parameter from post → DM → WhatsApp → order.
- Public comments and DMs are a future CRM queue, but there is no current response SLA/owner/next-action view.

## Channel utilisation audit

### Already present

- Instagram profile, Reels and carousels.
- WhatsApp catalogue, 1:1 handoff and a WhatsApp Community link on the site.
- Shopify storefront, email capture, blog/topic queue and Reddit listener inside Labs OS.
- Drop for product/build media and a documented Instagram → WhatsApp community playbook.

### Underused or missing

- **YouTube/short-form video library:** no connected YouTube account was available through Composio during this audit. Competitors use build explainers, consultations and watch education; connect the account only after the owner chooses the channel and access scope.
- **Search/SEO:** product names cover many homage terms, but the education layer needs indexed guides targeting “what is a Seiko mod,” “NH35 vs VK63,” “custom watch warranty,” “how long a custom watch takes” and “water resistance.”
- **Email lifecycle:** capture exists, but the audit could not verify a welcome, abandoned-consultation, post-delivery or referral sequence. These should be measured separately from one-off broadcasts.
- **Community listening:** Reddit listener exists, but the competitive workflow should turn recurring objections into content briefs and FAQ updates, never astroturfing or evading blocks.
- **Measurement:** one naming convention for campaign/content/source is needed across Instagram, WhatsApp, Shopify and Labs OS.
- **Creator/collector seeding:** no systematic public programme was found. Use a small number of documented owners/enthusiasts with disclosure, not anonymous review claims.

## 30/60/90-day action plan

### First 30 days — repair the funnel

1. Fix storefront typos, duplicate testimonials and product-level non-affiliation/water-resistance copy.
2. Add a build-passport block to every hero product: movement, components, QC date/result, maker, lead time and warranty boundary.
3. Replace the raw Instagram-to-WhatsApp link with a measured landing page and prefilled source-aware handoff.
4. Publish three repeatable Reel series: finished reveal, bench/process, and buyer education.
5. Define a WhatsApp/DM CRM record: source, product, budget, question, owner, next action, SLA and outcome.

### Days 31–60 — build demand and proof

1. Ship one educational SEO article per week and repurpose each into a carousel, Reel and WhatsApp Community post.
2. Launch a monthly “build slots” drop only when capacity and inventory support the claim.
3. Ask every delivered owner for permissioned wrist photo + structured review; link it to the build record.
4. Run a small creator/collector pilot with clear disclosure and UTM/source codes.
5. Connect and audit YouTube only if the owner wants to invest in long-form/video search.

### Days 61–90 — scale what converts

1. Compare Reel reach → WhatsApp starts → qualified consultations → deposits → delivered orders by collection.
2. Retire low-proof/low-margin catalogue clutter and promote a small number of original named collections.
3. Add post-delivery referral and service reminders to email/WhatsApp, with opt-out and no spam.
4. Review competitor price, stock, offer and policy changes weekly; update the dashboard every two days for first-party Instagram and weekly for public market observations.

## Measurement contract

The dashboard should track these definitions consistently:

- **Discovery:** Reel views, unique reach, profile visits, source-tagged landing sessions.
- **Intent:** DMs, WhatsApp chats started, qualified consultations, saved/shared content.
- **Commercial:** deposits, paid orders, conversion rate by source, gross margin after supplier cost.
- **Trust:** build-passport views, warranty questions, defect/repair rate, review completion and referral rate.
- **Operations:** response SLA, build lead time, photo/QC completion and delivery exception rate.

Do not use follower count as the primary success metric. The winning loop is profitable, traceable consultation-to-order conversion with fewer avoidable trust questions.

## Evidence and caveats

Public competitor pages are self-published marketing material and can change. Reddit/community posts are anecdotal and are not verified customer records. Instagram competitor private reach, DMs and audience data are unavailable and were not bypassed. Hermes/Composio had an active TimeLabs Instagram connection, but Meta silently omitted several account-level series for the selected period. Exact competitor pricing, availability, review counts and handles should be rechecked before a commercial decision.
