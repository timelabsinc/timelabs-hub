# TimeLabs brand voice

## Character

Confident, concrete, collector-aware and calm. The writing should feel like a knowledgeable independent maker explaining a good build—not a luxury conglomerate, a dropshipper or a hype account.

## Use

- Specific movement, component, finish, fit and ownership details.
- Short sentences and clean structure.
- Aspirational language grounded in observable craft.
- Plain disclosure about custom construction and non-affiliation.
- Soft, useful calls to action: explore the collection, compare specifications, ask about a build.

## Avoid

- “Introducing,” “elevate,” “timeless elegance,” “game-changing,” “revolutionary,” and generic luxury filler.
- Unsupported superlatives, fake scarcity or arbitrary countdowns.
- Pretending aftermarket/custom parts are factory Seiko products.
- Aggressive “buy now” language, excessive emoji or hashtag stuffing.
- Copy that sounds machine-generated, repetitive or detached from the actual watch.

## Working phrases

- Hand-built, not mass-produced.
- Seiko-powered, TimeLabs-crafted.
- From our creation to your collection.
- Built around the details you chose.

These are working phrases, not mandatory slogans. Use them only when they fit the context and avoid repeating them across every surface.

## Proof hierarchy

Prefer, in order:

1. Current first-party measurement or build record.
2. Observable component/specification.
3. Permissioned customer evidence.
4. Clearly labelled inference.

Never convert a competitor claim, community anecdote or missing metric into a TimeLabs fact.
