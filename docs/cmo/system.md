# CMO workspace system

## Operating model

Hermes is the single accountable CMO and coordinator. The visible specialist agents are focused playbooks—not separate personalities and not independent unreviewed bots. Each specialist receives the same company documents, current business data and approval policy, then returns a scoped brief or prepares an internal task.

## Context layer

- Product Information
- Marketing Strategy
- Competitive Intelligence
- Brand Voice
- Human Writing Standard
- Current Labs OS state and business memory
- First-party sales, product, Instagram and operational data

Documents are versioned in the repository. Current metrics are read from their systems of record and must not be copied into permanent documents as if they were timeless.

## Specialist layer

- Instagram
- Content
- SEO/GEO
- Reddit/community
- WhatsApp/email
- UGC
- Influencer/collector partnerships
- Competitor intelligence
- Storefront conversion
- Analytics/measurement

## Coordination layer

Hermes combines specialist findings into a weekly CMO decision memo, resolves conflicts against commercial priorities and records approved decisions/tasks. One specialist cannot publish or spend merely because another specialist recommended it.

## Action layer

- Inspect and analyse: direct when authorised.
- Draft and prepare internal work: direct.
- Publish, message, spend, discount, contact competitors or change Shopify: owner approval and the existing Labs OS tool boundary.

## Current limitation

Command conversations can preserve long results, but Labs OS does not yet have a generic persistent multi-agent run engine with typed jobs, progress, dependencies, retry, cancellation and restart recovery. The UI therefore describes specialists as ready workflows and sends their briefs through Hermes; it must not imply ten autonomous background workers are running continuously.
