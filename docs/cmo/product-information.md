# Product Information

## Overview

**Product name:** TimeLabs Co  
**Website:** https://timelabsco.in  
**One-liner:** TimeLabs Co sells hand-built custom Seiko-movement watches across a range of mod collections, shipped globally.

## What it does

TimeLabs Co is an e-commerce watch brand that designs and hand-builds custom-modded watches powered by authentic Seiko movements. Customers browse curated collections with distinct dial styles, bezels and colourways and purchase through the Shopify store. The brand ships across India and internationally, positioning itself as a boutique alternative to mass-produced watches.

## Product category

- Custom watch store
- Seiko-movement mod watches
- Accessible luxury e-commerce

**Product type:** E-commerce

## Target customers

Watch enthusiasts, mod collectors and style-conscious buyers in India and global diaspora markets who want a luxury-aesthetic timepiece at an accessible price.
