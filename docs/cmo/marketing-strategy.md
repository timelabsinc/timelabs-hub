# TimeLabs Co — Marketing Strategy

## Ideal customer profile

### Primary: informed watch enthusiast

- **Age:** 22–38
- **Location:** Indian tier-one cities plus UAE, UK, US and Singapore diaspora markets
- **Income hypothesis:** ₹6L–₹20L annually; validate before paid targeting
- **Mindset:** researches watches, follows YouTube/Reddit/Instagram, owns a small collection, values craft and specification
- **Pain:** luxury references are out of budget while mass-market designs feel generic
- **Trigger:** a dial, case or collection identity that feels personally distinctive

### Secondary: style-conscious gift buyer

- Typically 28–45 and buying for a partner or milestone
- Needs confidence, delivery certainty, premium presentation and simple guidance more than enthusiast terminology

## Positioning

For watch enthusiasts and style-conscious buyers who want an individual, premium-looking timepiece without traditional luxury pricing, TimeLabs Co hand-builds custom Seiko-movement watches in distinctive collections and supports the customer through selection, build and ownership.

The defensible distinction is not merely “luxury look for less.” It is documented components, transparent custom construction, proof-led quality control, responsive consultation and an ownership promise.

## Messaging framework

| Layer | Working message |
|---|---|
| Brand line | From our creation to your collection |
| Build | Hand-built, not mass-produced |
| Provenance | Seiko-powered, TimeLabs-crafted |
| Choice | Distinct collections and colourways without a generic retail experience |
| Trust | Clear components, QC, warranty, delivery and aftercare |
| Primary CTA | Shop all mods or start a consultation |
| Voice | Confident, collector-aware, specific and accessible |

Do not use claims such as satisfaction percentages, “authentic,” tested water resistance or customer counts externally unless the current evidence is available and linked to the claim.

## Channel priorities

1. **Instagram/Reels:** discovery, build reveals, bench process, owner proof and objections.
2. **WhatsApp:** high-intent consultation and customer attention queue, with source tracking and consent.
3. **Shopify/search:** proof-rich product pages plus educational search content.
4. **Email:** welcome, consultation follow-up, aftercare, review and referral lifecycle.
5. **Reddit/watch communities:** authentic expertise and listening; never covert promotion.
6. **YouTube:** build education and collection walkthroughs when production capacity exists.
7. **Paid Meta:** only after Pixel/events, source attribution, creative baseline and stop/scale rules are verified.

## 30-day operating direction

### Foundation

- Fix product proof, disclosure, copy and first-time-buyer navigation.
- Measure Instagram/landing/WhatsApp/order handoffs.
- Define build-passport/QC content.

### Content system

- Produce repeatable finished-build, bench/process and education Reel series.
- Pair discovery Reels with proof carousels and source-aware consultation CTAs.
- Repurpose evidence-backed topics across WhatsApp, email and search.

### Conversion and retention

- Define the DM/WhatsApp attention record: source, product, intent, owner, next action, SLA and outcome.
- Implement permissioned review/wrist-shot collection after delivery.
- Add welcome, aftercare, review and referral lifecycle messages with opt-out.

### Review

- Compare reach and saves/shares to qualified consultations, orders and contribution margin.
- Scale only creative/channel combinations with known-source commercial outcomes.
