# Creator guide

This workspace helps you turn real photos and confirmed facts into content that
is ready to review and paste into the correct channel. It prepares drafts. It
does not post, send messages or publish anything for you.

## The simple workflow

1. **Start a new chat for each content item.** This keeps facts from another
   watch, customer or campaign out of the new draft.
2. **Attach the source.** Add the photos, screenshot, product link, customer
   question or rough notes the content must use.
3. **Choose the channel.** Use one of the buttons on the Creator desk. A short
   form will appear in the message box.
4. **Fill what you know.** Rough notes are fine. Never guess a price, movement,
   material, delivery date, warranty or water resistance. Leave it blank if it
   has not been confirmed.
5. **Send it to Hermes.** If an essential fact is missing, Hermes should ask one
   short question. Otherwise it will return one or more ready-to-paste blocks.
6. **Review, copy and publish manually.** Use **Copy this text** on the block you
   want. Paste it into the platform, add the correct media/link, preview it and
   publish only after the final check below.

## What to give Hermes

The best brief usually contains:

- the purpose: showcase, review, answer, announcement, sale or education;
- what is visible in the photo or video;
- confirmed product facts that the image cannot show;
- who the content is for;
- the one action the reader should take, if any;
- words, claims or details that must not be used.

You do not need to write complete sentences. Short factual notes are better
than polished but uncertain copy.

## Pick the right route

### Instagram caption

Use for a Reel, carousel or product photo. Supply the content goal, what the
media shows, confirmed watch facts, one behind-the-scenes detail and the desired
next step. Hermes returns two caption options in separate copy blocks.

### Instagram Story

Use when you need a short sequence of Story frames. Supply the number and order
of photos/clips, the purpose of the sequence and any poll, question or link you
want. Each frame is returned separately so it is easy to paste.

### Reddit

Use for a showcase, build diary, honest review, useful answer or founder note.
State the subreddit, your relationship to TimeLabs and what value the post gives
the community. Open the thread first when replying. Do not hide the brand
relationship or turn an answer into an advertisement.

### WhatsApp

Use for a customer reply, community update or follow-up. Paste the actual
situation, the confirmed facts and the one next step. Do not paste customer
phone numbers, payment details or other unnecessary private information.

### Sales reply

Paste what the buyer asked and only the confirmed price, availability,
specification and delivery information relevant to the answer. Hermes should
answer the question first and avoid pressure or fake scarcity.

### Email

State who the email is for, why it is being sent, the facts to include and the
requested next step. Subject options and the email body are returned as separate
copy blocks.

### Blog

Give Hermes the question the article must answer, first-party evidence and
openable source links. Do not use a statistic or quotation without its source.
The article is returned in a Markdown block for the blog editor.

### YouTube

Supply the topic, available footage, verified facts, target length and desired
next step. The block contains the spoken script and simple shot cues.

### Ads, product copy, LinkedIn and founder posts

Type `/ad`, `/product`, `/linkedin` or `/founder` before the brief. Ads require
proof for every claim. Product copy requires confirmed specifications. LinkedIn
and founder posts require a real decision, mistake, customer moment or number.

## Final check before publishing

- Does every claim match the source, product record or confirmed message?
- Does the copy match the attached photo/video and the selected platform?
- Is the TimeLabs or founder relationship disclosed where the audience needs it?
- Are the price, link, tagged account, spelling and call to action correct?
- Did you remove internal notes and paste only the content block?
- Does it sound natural when read aloud?

If any answer is **no** or **not sure**, ask Hermes to revise the draft or send it
to the owner for review. Never repair a missing fact by guessing.

## Useful follow-up requests

- `Make this shorter without changing any facts.`
- `Give me one calmer option. Keep the same point.`
- `Check every claim against the notes I supplied.`
- `Adapt the approved caption for WhatsApp. Do not add new claims.`
- `Explain what is still missing before this can be published.`

Keep approvals in the chat. Once the owner approves a final version, copy that
version rather than asking Hermes to recreate it from memory.
